Replace the current MyDraft billing system with a complete credit-based economy.

GOAL

The goal is to prevent AI abuse, control AI costs, increase profitability, and create additional revenue through credit purchases while keeping the system simple and easy for users to understand.

CREDIT SYSTEM

Every AI action must consume credits.

Credit Costs:

* AI Reply = 2 credits
* AI Compose = 2 credits
* AI Summary = 1 credit
* AI Rewrite = 1 credit
* Inbox Cleanup = dynamic based on the number of emails scanned
* Bulk AI Actions = dynamic based on workload

Inbox Cleanup includes:

* Spam detection
* Scam detection
* Junk identification
* Email organization
* Cleanup suggestions

Do NOT charge separately for spam or scam scanning.

Before any AI action:

1. Check available credits.
2. Calculate required credits.
3. If enough credits exist:

   * Deduct credits.
   * Log the transaction.
   * Run the action.
4. If not enough credits exist:

   * Block the action.
   * Show an upgrade/purchase modal.

Users must never be able to have a negative credit balance.

MONTHLY CREDIT ADD-ONS

CREDIT EXPIRATION RULES

All credits expire exactly 30 days after being added to the account regardless of source.

This includes:

* Subscription credits
* Purchased credits
* Add-on credits
* Referral credits

Always use the oldest credits first.

Display expiration dates clearly to users.

REFERRAL PROGRAM

Add a referral system.

Referrer Reward:

* 25 credits

Referred User Reward:

* 1 month of Pro for free

Requirements:

* User creates an account.
* User verifies their email.
* User connects an email account.
* No self-referrals.
* One reward per unique user.
* Prevent duplicate and fraudulent referrals.

When requirements are met:

* Grant 25 credits to the referrer.
* Grant 1 month of Pro access to the referred user.

Referral credits expire after 30 days.
