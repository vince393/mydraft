## 30-minute monthly ritual

Once a month, spend ~5 min per competitor:

1. Open the pricing page — did tiers, prices, or the free plan change?
2. Skim the changelog/blog — any big new feature (especially AI)?
3. Glance at 1–3 star G2 reviews — any repeated complaint you could win on?
4. Check LinkedIn jobs — hiring signals what they're about to build.
5. Log anything notable in the change log below and note your action.

**On-demand:** paste this back to the assistant anytime — *"Run my monthly competitive check for [competitor name]."*
